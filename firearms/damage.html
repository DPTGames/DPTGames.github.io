Damage Integration

Reusable hit interface · Health, UnityEvents, physics responses and optional destruction integrations

A shared contract for both shot modes and bayonet hits

All ballistic damage is handled by the DPT_FirearmProjectile impact path for both Hitscan and Swept Projectile. The optional bayonet delivers damage through the same IDPT_FirearmDamageable interface. You can use the provided relay or implement your own health receiver. Optional penetration and ricochets may deliver separate contact hits along one projectile path; later contacts use reduced damage as projectile speed falls.

Shared Damage Contract

using UnityEngine;

namespace DPT.Firearms.Runtime
{
    public interface IDPT_FirearmDamageable
    {
        void ReceiveFirearmHit(float damage, Vector3 point,
            Vector3 direction, GameObject source);
    }
}
The projectile and bayonet query this interface on the struck Collider's parent hierarchy. point is the world-space impact position and direction is the incoming attack direction. Configure bullet damage on the DPT_FirearmProjectile prefab, not on the firearm.

DPT_FirearmDamageRelay

  1. Add DPT_FirearmDamageRelay to the target or a parent of its Collider.
  2. Connect On Damage to a UnityEvent listener that accepts a float, or use On Hit for parameterless reactions.
  3. For more information, read LastDamage, LastHitPoint, LastHitDirection and LastSource, or subscribe to the Hit C# event.

Implementing a Custom Health Receiver

using DPT.Firearms.Runtime;
using UnityEngine;

public class ExampleFirearmHealth : MonoBehaviour, IDPT_FirearmDamageable
{
    [SerializeField] private float health = 100f;

    public void ReceiveFirearmHit(float damage, Vector3 point,
        Vector3 direction, GameObject source)
    {
        health = Mathf.Max(0f, health - damage);
        if (health == 0f)
        {
            gameObject.SetActive(false);
        }
    }
}

Penetration, Ricochet and Multi-Contact Damage

When the optional DPT_FirearmSurfaceImpact component allows a bullet to continue through a surface or ricochet, DPT_FirearmProjectile processes each subsequent contact using the same IDPT_FirearmDamageable interface. The damage amount is scaled by the current projectile speed relative to its original muzzle velocity; physics impact impulses scale down similarly.

The Impacted(Vector3) C# event fires for every contact (penetration entry, ricochet or final stop). By contrast, the onImpact UnityEvent fires only when the projectile comes to rest. Gameplay listeners that must react to every impact should subscribe to Impacted or use the target damage interface.

Damage receivers: A bullet can contact more than one target during a single shot when continuation is enabled. Your damage receiver should decide how to handle repeated contacts if the same object may be struck again.

Example: Shoot a Moving Primitive Out of the Air

  • Add a Collider, Rigidbody and a receiver implementing IDPT_FirearmDamageable to the moving target.
  • If a target moves under scripted control, use a kinematic Rigidbody and drive it with MovePosition() during FixedUpdate(). When hit, the receiver can disable scripted movement, enable gravity and make the body non-kinematic.
  • Physics Impact Impulse now lives on the projectile prefab and applies to both Hitscan and Swept Projectile. The firearm no longer has a separate Hitscan Impact Impulse.
  • Bayonet Impact Impulse remains on the firearm and applies only to bayonet strikes. If the damage receiver also applies forces, set the appropriate projectile or bayonet impulse to zero to prevent stacking.

Damage Troubleshooting

IssueCheck
Bullet passes through targetProjectile's Collision Layers, collider enabled state, raycast direction and trigger settings.
Impact effect plays but no health is removedThe struck Collider or one of its parents must implement IDPT_FirearmDamageable.
Target moves with excessive forceCheck projectile Physics Impact Impulse, bayonet impact impulse and any force applied by the damage receiver. Hitscan uses the projectile impulse.
Crushed remnant detaches from a moving targetCheck whether the collider is replaced or destroyed by the target's damage response. Parenting to the original struck collider cannot automatically follow a replacement object.
Script cannot see firearm interfacesReference the DPT.Firearms.Runtime assembly and import its namespace.
Bayonet event fires without damageAn eligible blade collision may occur without a damage receiver on the struck target.
Penetration or ricochet does not damage a second targetCheck the projectile Collision Layers, the surface rule, retained speed and remaining range. Every struck target needs IDPT_FirearmDamageable on the collider or a parent.
Impact event appears to fire several timesThe projectile Impacted C# event intentionally fires for each contact. The onImpact UnityEvent is reserved for the final stop.
