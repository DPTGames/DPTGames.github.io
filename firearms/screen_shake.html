Camera, Screen Shake & Procedural Recoil

Optional camera effects · Firing shake, per-contact impact shake and reusable procedural recoil

Two senders, one optional camera receiver

The firearm sender broadcasts firing shake. The projectile sender broadcasts impact shake from the projectile's Impacted event in both Hitscan and Swept Projectile modes. The receiver attenuates shake by distance. No firearm-level hitscan impact-shake fields are required. Standalone recoil is a separate optional component that responds to successful shots rather than impact events.

Quick Setup

  1. Add DPT_FirearmFireScreenShake to the weapon GameObject for shot-fired shake.
  2. Add DPT_FirearmProjectileScreenShake to the projectile prefab for impact shake. The same prefab is responsible for impacts in both shot modes.
  3. Add DPT_FirearmCameraShakeReceiver to Main Camera or an isolated camera-effects pivot. Use one receiver per camera.
  4. In the firearm's custom Inspector, tune the optional firing sender under Screen Shake. Tune projectile impact Magnitude, Duration and Radius on the projectile prefab itself.

How Events Are Routed

Firing: DPT_Firearm.ShotFired → Fire sender → OnFireShake → Camera receiver
Impact: DPT_FirearmProjectile.Impacted → Projectile sender → OnImpactShake → Camera receiver

A Hitscan hit creates a projectile impact instance and invokes its shared contact handler; a miss has no projectile impact event. With optional surface rules, penetration entries and ricochets each raise Impacted, which can trigger additional impact shake. The projectile onImpact UnityEvent is separate and fires on a final stop.

DPT_FirearmCameraShakeReceiver

  • Magnitude Multiplier scales firing and impact shake received by that camera. The effective strength falls with distance to the muzzle or impact point.
  • The receiver replaces a weaker shake with a stronger incoming one rather than indefinitely stacking offsets.
  • It applies a local positional offset in LateUpdate with a high execution order; it does not perform rotational weapon recoil.
  • Use one receiver per camera and test compatibility when another camera system also writes to the camera Transform.

Standalone Procedural Recoil

DPT_FirearmRecoil is an optional runtime component that listens to DPT_Firearm.ShotFired and applies local translation and rotation to a configured Recoil Target. Unlike DPT_FirearmCameraShakeReceiver, it produces intentional shot kick with a configurable recovery curve, independent of camera distance from an impact.

  1. On your firearm, open Procedural Recoil and select Add Procedural Recoil, or add DPT_FirearmRecoil directly to the same weapon GameObject.
  2. Assign a Recoil Target, such as the Main Camera, a weapon pivot or preferably a dedicated child pivot for recoil.
  3. Set local Position Kick and Rotation Kick per shot. Adjust Maximum Position Offset, Maximum Rotation Offset, Recovery Speed and Recoil Multiplier.
  4. Fire repeatedly and verify that impulses accumulate up to the configured caps then return smoothly. The module is inactive visually until a Recoil Target is assigned.

Demo controller compatibility: DPT_FirearmDemoInput keeps its built-in camera recoil when this module is absent or has no target. When an enabled DPT_FirearmRecoil has a target, the demo suppresses its own visual recoil to avoid doubling the effect.
Suggested hierarchy: CameraRig → RecoilPivot → Camera. Assign RecoilPivot as the module's target and place the camera shake receiver on the Camera or another dedicated effects pivot. Test how the pivots interact with the rest of your controller.

Camera-only Demo Integration

Keep the demo's Main Camera outside the rifle hierarchy. Attach the receiver to the camera or an effects pivot, and avoid multiple receivers on the same Transform.
