DPT Firearm Camera & Screen Shake

Modular Camera Effects Guide
Three optional components transmit shot and impact shake through static events and apply distance-attenuated positional offsets to a camera or camera pivot.
Quick Navigation Reference:Quick SetupEvent FlowFire Shake SettingsProjectile Shake SettingsCamera ReceiverTroubleshooting

Quick Setup

  1. Add DPT_FirearmFireScreenShake to the same GameObject as the DPT_Firearm component.
  2. Add DPT_FirearmProjectileScreenShake to the bullet prefab if you want moving-projectile impacts to trigger shake.
  3. Add DPT_FirearmCameraShakeReceiver to the player camera or a dedicated child pivot that moves the camera.
  4. Set the fire/impact magnitudes, durations and radii. These are positional offsets in Unity units, not degrees of camera rotation.
  5. Ensure the GameObjects carrying the components are active and the impact layer masks allow the projectile or hitscan shot to detect surfaces.
Avoid duplicate receivers: Using the original siege weapon camera-shake receiver alongside the firearm receiver can stack two independently applied offsets. Prefer a single deliberate shake system per camera.

How The Screen Shake Modules Communicate

Firearm Shot → ShotFired → Fire Shake Broadcast → Camera Receiver
Projectile Hit → Impacted → Impact Shake Broadcast → Camera Receiver

The shake sender broadcasts an impact position, magnitude, duration and radius via static C# events. Each receiver computes a distance-based attenuation. The receiver favours a stronger incoming shake over its current remaining shake rather than summing every source into an ever-growing offset.

DPT_FirearmFireScreenShake

Requires DPT_Firearm on the same GameObject. It subscribes to ShotFired and HitOccurred.

Firing

PropertyDescription
Fire Magnitude
fireMagnitude		Maximum camera-position shake offset caused by firing, in local Unity distance units before distance attenuation.
Fire Duration
fireDuration		Duration of the firing camera shake, in seconds.
Fire Radius
fireRadius		Maximum distance in metres from the muzzle at which a camera can receive firing shake.

Hitscan Impact

PropertyDescription
Shake On Hitscan Impact
shakeOnHitscanImpact		Also broadcasts camera shake when this firearm's Hitscan shot hits a surface.
Impact Magnitude
impactMagnitude		Maximum camera-position shake offset at a Hitscan impact, before distance attenuation.
Impact Duration
impactDuration		Duration of Hitscan impact shake, in seconds.
Impact Radius
impactRadius		Maximum distance in metres from a Hitscan impact at which cameras receive shake.
Hitscan only: The optional Shake On Hitscan Impact uses DPT_Firearm.HitOccurred. Swept projectile impacts require the separate projectile impact shake component.

DPT_FirearmProjectileScreenShake

Requires DPT_FirearmProjectile on the same GameObject. It subscribes to the projectile's Impacted event and emits impact shake with its own magnitude, duration and distance radius.

Fields

PropertyDescription
Magnitude
magnitude		Maximum camera-position shake offset caused by this projectile's impact, before distance attenuation.
Duration
duration		Duration of the projectile impact shake, in seconds.
Radius
radius		Maximum distance in metres from the impact at which cameras receive shake.

DPT_FirearmCameraShakeReceiver

The receiver listens to both shake broadcast events while enabled and offsets its own Transform.localPosition in LateUpdate(). It retains a base position and attempts to avoid cumulative positional drift as other scripts move the camera.

Fields

PropertyDescription
Magnitude Multiplier
magnitudeMultiplier		Scales all firearm firing and projectile impact shake received by this camera. Set to zero to disable positional shake.
Suggested camera setup: Use a dedicated Shake_Pivot child of your normal camera controller if another script continually writes the camera's local position or you already use recoil and head-bob. This keeps those systems easier to isolate.

Troubleshooting

SymptomCheck
No shake when firingCheck that both the firearm fire shake sender and camera receiver are enabled. Verify the camera is inside Fire Radius.
No shake when a moving bullet impactsAdd DPT_FirearmProjectileScreenShake to the same prefab as the projectile component.
Too much camera movementReduce magnitudes, especially if you have another recoil, head-bob or siege shake receiver.
Shake not visible on distant shotsShake is distance-attenuated and is zero outside the assigned radius.
Shake unexpectedly offsets scripted camera movementPut the receiver on a child Shake Pivot that is not overwritten by your camera controller.
