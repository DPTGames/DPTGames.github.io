Camera & Screen Shake

Optional effects guide · Firearm impulses, impact broadcasts and distance-attenuated camera reactions

Optional camera shake for shots and impacts

Three lightweight scripts broadcast firearm firing and projectile impact events and apply local camera-position offsets with distance attenuation. They can be omitted entirely if your project uses its own camera effects.

On this pageQuick Setup Event Flow Sender Settings Receiver Demo Integration

Quick Setup

  1. Add DPT_FirearmFireScreenShake to the firearm GameObject.
  2. Optionally add DPT_FirearmProjectileScreenShake to the bullet prefab for projectile-impact shake.
  3. Attach DPT_FirearmCameraShakeReceiver to the Main Camera or a dedicated camera pivot.
  4. Tune the magnitudes, durations and radii in the Inspector. Use one receiver per camera.

How Events Are Routed

ShotFired→Fire sender→OnFireShake→Camera receiver
Impacted→Projectile sender→OnImpactShake→Camera receiver

Sender Components

ComponentFields / purpose
DPT_FirearmFireScreenShakeFire Magnitude, Fire Duration and Fire Radius. Can optionally shake on Hitscan impact using Shake On Hitscan Impact plus Impact Magnitude/Duration/Radius.
DPT_FirearmProjectileScreenShakeMagnitude, Duration and Radius for individual swept projectile hits.
BroadcastsThe C# events carry world impact position, magnitude, duration and radius to the receiver.

DPT_FirearmCameraShakeReceiver

  • Magnitude Multiplier scales incoming shake for that camera.
  • Shake strength falls with distance. When a new stronger shake arrives, it replaces the remaining weaker one rather than adding unlimited offsets.
  • The receiver uses positional movement, not rotational recoil; the demo controller handles its own camera recoil separately.
  • The receiver is marked with a late execution order so it can apply shake after other camera scripts. Test with custom cinematic camera systems before release.

Camera-only Demo Integration

The demo camera is not parented to the rifle; keep it that way. Attach the shake receiver directly to Main Camera or a camera-effects pivot. Do not attach two receivers to the same camera, as their offsets can stack.
SymptomLikely cause
No shakeReceiver is missing or disabled, radius is too small or the sender is not attached.
Excessive shakeMultiple receivers, a high Magnitude Multiplier or overlapping camera effects.
Camera flickers during shakeTwo scripts may be writing camera position in an incompatible order.
Hit shake plays twiceBoth firearm hitscan shake and another gameplay effect may be active.
