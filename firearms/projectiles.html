DPT Firearm Projectile

Moving Bullet & Impact Guide
The DPT_FirearmProjectile component moves fast projectiles using swept raycasts, applies damage on the first solid impact and optionally swaps an intact bullet mesh for a crushed or deformed variant.

Quick Setup

  1. Create a new bullet prefab. Add DPT_FirearmProjectile to its root GameObject.
  2. Under that root, create a child GameObject for the normal projectile mesh and optionally a second child for the crushed or deformed mesh.
  3. Assign both visual GameObjects to Normal Bullet Visual and Deformed Bullet Visual respectively.
  4. Configure the projectile's Collision Layers, gravity, drag, impact effect, impact sound and remnant lifetime.
  5. Assign this prefab to Projectile Prefab on DPT_Firearm and set Shot Type to SweptProjectile.
  6. For impact shake, optionally attach DPT_FirearmProjectileScreenShake to the same prefab.

No flight Rigidbody required: The projectile uses a swept raycast in FixedUpdate() to detect collision between its previous and next positions. If the root has a Rigidbody, the script makes it kinematic in Awake(). It also does not require a Collider for the flight raycasts.

Hitscan vs Swept Projectiles

ModeBehaviour
HitscanThe firearm's PerformHitscan() checks for an immediate impact. No projectile prefab is spawned. Hitscan effects, damage and impulse are controlled by DPT_Firearm.
Swept ProjectileThe firearm instantiates DPT_FirearmProjectile at the muzzle, calls Initialize(), then the projectile advances using gravity/drag and raycasts along each movement segment.
What happens at 780 m/s?A conventional Rigidbody collision can tunnel through thin objects between physics steps. The swept path uses a raycast from the previous position across the full step distance, which mitigates that problem.

Projectile Lifecycle & Deformed Bullet

Instantiate → Initialize → Swept Flight → First Hit → Impact Visual → Destroy
  1. Initialize(source, velocity, damage, maxDistance) sets the source firearm, initial velocity, damage and allowed range. The normal bullet visual becomes active and the deformed one is hidden.
  2. Every physics step, the projectile applies optional gravity and a simple linear drag approximation, then raycasts along the traveled segment.
  3. The closest eligible hit is selected. The projectile stops at the first hit, applies damage via IDPT_FirearmDamageable and optional force to a non-kinematic Rigidbody.
  4. It spawns the impact effect, plays one randomly selected impact clip and optionally switches from the intact bullet to the crushed bullet.
  5. The projectile disables its child Colliders, optionally attaches to the struck transform and destroys itself after Lifetime After Impact.
Impact offset: In this implementation, Embed Depth shifts the stopped bullet backwards along its incoming direction, away from the surface hit point. Increasing this number makes the visible remnant sit further outside the target rather than penetrate farther into it.

Performance & Known Limitations

  • The moving projectile performs one non-allocating physics raycast operation per FixedUpdate, checking up to 64 returned hits to choose a valid nearest collision.
  • The projectile ignores trigger colliders, its own children and colliders in the launching firearm's hierarchy.
  • Motion simulation uses a simple linear drag approximation. It is not a full ballistic coefficient or aerodynamic spin model.
  • The projectile stops at the first solid surface. No penetration, ricochet or continuation through destructible meshes is implemented.
  • Impact effects and audio use instantiation/playback rather than pooling. High-volume automatic weapons may benefit from pooling added by the consuming project.
  • With Attach To Hit Transform enabled, the remnant follows a moving target while it exists. If that target is removed or replaced by a mesh-destruction system, additional handling may be needed.

Troubleshooting

SymptomCheck
Projectile is invisibleConfirm the prefab has a normal bullet visual assigned, the mesh renderer is active and the projectile is travelling in the camera view.
Crushed bullet never showsEnable Show Deformed Bullet On Impact, assign the deformed child and verify a hit was registered.
Bullet flies through everythingCheck the Collision Layers mask, whether the colliders are triggers and whether objects are excluded as part of the firearm’s hierarchy.
Projectile disappears before hittingIncrease Maximum Range on the firearm or Fallback Lifetime on the projectile.
Impact audio not audibleAssign Impact Sounds; remember the default Impact Volume is 0.5.
Projectile stays attached after target breaksTurn off Attach To Hit Transform or provide an integration that detaches the remnant before the target collider is replaced.
