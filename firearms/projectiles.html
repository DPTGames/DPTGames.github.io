Projectiles & Bullet Impacts

Generic projectile guide · Hitscan, swept motion, gravity, damaged bullets and impact events

Instant hits or physically simulated bullet travel

The generic firearm supports immediate hitscan raycasts and a lightweight swept-projectile component. The included .303 example illustrates the latter, but projectile speed, damage, gravity and visuals are configurable for any firearm.

Projectile Prefab Setup

  1. Create a bullet prefab and add DPT_FirearmProjectile to its root.
  2. Create child objects for an intact bullet mesh and optional crushed/deformed impact mesh. Assign each under the projectile’s Impact settings.
  3. Assign the prefab to Projectile Prefab on the firearm and select Swept Projectile.
  4. Configure Collision Layers, Gravity Multiplier, Linear Drag, Fallback Lifetime, Impact Sounds and the impact effects.
  5. Place and orient Muzzle Point on the firearm; the bullet spawns at that Transform.

Hitscan vs Swept Projectile

ModeHow it works
HitscanOne immediate raycast from the muzzle. No moving projectile prefab is spawned. Uses firearm Hit Layers and Hitscan Impact Impulse.
Swept ProjectileInstantiates a projectile at the muzzle, integrates motion over FixedUpdate and raycasts the entire travelled segment each physics step. Supports gravity, drag, remnants and impact events.

Impact Flow

Spawn→Initialize→Swept motion→First hit→Damage + effect→Remnant / destroy
  • On first valid impact, the projectile stops and applies configured damage through IDPT_FirearmDamageable.
  • If a non-kinematic Rigidbody is hit and Physics Impact Impulse is nonzero, an impulse is applied in the incoming direction.
  • If configured, the intact bullet mesh is hidden and the deformed visual is shown for the remainder lifetime.
  • The projectile can parent itself to the struck Transform so the remnant follows a moving object.

Aiming and Ballistics

  • The bullet is emitted from the muzzle, not directly from the camera. This distinction matters for nearby objects and sight alignment.
  • The optional demo controller can converge the muzzle direction to a ray cast from the camera centre, but actual swept projectiles still follow gravity after launch.
  • For calibration, test Hitscan, Spread Degrees = 0 and an ADS camera pose aligned with the iron sights. Then re-enable gravity to evaluate projectile drop.
  • The current sample does not provide a dedicated ballistic zero-distance solver. Do not present the demo’s crosshair convergence as full physical sight zeroing.

Performance Notes

  • Swept raycasts reduce fast-projectile tunnelling but are still physics queries each FixedUpdate.
  • The projectile uses non-allocating raycasts, but prefab instantiation, impact effects and casing creation still allocate. Pooling is a later optimisation option.
  • A Rigidbody is not needed for the flight simulation. If present, the projectile makes it kinematic in Awake.
  • Do not apply duplicate impulses in both the projectile and the target’s damage handler unless intentional.
