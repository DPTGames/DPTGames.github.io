Projectiles & Impacts

Shot modes, ammo profile overrides, surface reactions and damage

Set up the shot

The weapon chooses the projectile from the round it fires. The projectile prefab controls range, flight, damage and impact effects. Use a shared prefab for consistent behaviour or override it in an ammo profile.

Quick Setup

  1. Duplicate a supplied projectile such as 303_Bullet or Buckshot, or add DPT_FirearmProjectile to a new prefab with a Rigidbody on its root.
  2. Set Shot Type to Hitscan or Swept Projectile. Check the target layers and maximum range.
  3. Assign the prefab to Projectile Prefab on the firearm. To use a different projectile for a specific round, assign its Projectile Prefab in the ammo profile.
  4. Test in the range. For a target that should receive damage, add a compatible damage receiver on the hit object or a parent.
You can edit the simulation mode under Firearm > Projectiles & Impacts. The setting is saved on the projectile prefab. Other weapons and ammo profiles using the same prefab will pick up the change.

Hitscan & Swept Projectile

ModeWhat you seeHow the hit resolves
HitscanAn immediate hit with optional effects and an embedded remnant on a stopping surface.The firearm checks for the first hit along the shot ray. It creates a projectile instance on contact to handle damage, effects and any follow-up surface reactions.
Swept ProjectileA visible round in flight, with optional gravity and drag.Each physics step sweeps the distance travelled by the round. Hits are processed along that path.

The muzzle sets the shot origin. Spread comes from the loaded ammo profile or the firearm’s fallback value. If crosshair convergence is active, the base shot direction points from the muzzle towards the camera’s aim target before spread is applied.

The script manages flight and makes its Rigidbody kinematic. For a preview in the Scene view, enable the firearm’s range, trajectory or damage gizmos. The guides show the unobstructed shot.

Ammo Profile Settings

Use Create > DPT Games > Firearms > Ammunition Type to make a profile, or copy the weapon’s settings through Create Ammo Profile.

Profile fieldWhat it controls

Projectile PrefabOptional override. Leave it empty to use the firearm’s projectile.
Projectile Count / Spread DegreesHow many virtual projectiles one cartridge or shell releases and the half-angle of their spread cone.
Damage Multiplier / Velocity MultiplierScale the projectile’s damage per hit and initial speed.

Ammo reserves count shells or cartridges. One shell spends one round from reserve when loaded, even if it fires several pellets.

Surface Rules

Open Surface Interaction on the projectile prefab’s DPT_FirearmProjectile component. Layer Rules are checked in order. Put specific layers before general rules. A rule can apply to Any, Horizontal Only or Vertical Only surfaces, using Horizontal Dot Threshold to test the hit normal. A contact with no matching rule uses the default Stop reaction.

ReactionBehaviour
StopStop at the contact point and show the final impact if configured.
PenetrateContinue when the projectile meets the minimum speed and can find an exit within the maximum thickness. Apply the configured velocity retention after the pass.
RicochetReflect from the surface when speed and angle meet the rule. The angle is measured from the surface normal, so higher values require a shallower contact.
AutomaticTry ricochet first when eligible, then penetration. Stop if neither can run.

Max Penetrations, Max Ricochets and Maximum Contacts Per Step cap the work for one projectile. If a penetration test fails, check the collider’s shape and exit thickness.

Projectile Lifecycle & Remnants

Use Normal Bullet Visual for the model in flight and Deformed Bullet Visual for the optional stopped remnant. Enable Show Deformed Bullet On Impact to swap to it when the projectile stops. Embed Depth adjusts the final embed offset and Lifetime After Impact controls how long the stopped instance remains. Zero removes it on impact.

Each contact can trigger its own effects and Impacted event. A projectile that keeps travelling after a penetration or ricochet retains its flight visual until it stops or expires.

Impact Effects & Audio

  • Impact Effect Prefab spawns at the contact point and follows the hit object. Use it for dust, a mark or a composite effect. Impact Effect Lifetime caps the spawned effect lifetime.
  • Override Impact Effect on a surface rule selects a replacement effect for that surface. Enable it with an empty reference to suppress the default effect.
  • Spalling Effect Prefab adds a separate debris, dust or spark effect. Spalling Scale multiplies the prefab’s scale.
  • Impact Sounds and Impact Volume control the default audio. A surface rule can replace or mute the clips and apply a volume multiplier.
  • Under Impact on DPT_FirearmProjectile, assign an optional Impact Audio Source to use its mixer routing, pitch and spatial settings for playback at the hit point. Leave the field empty for default 3D playback. Choose the clips in Impact Sounds or in a Layer Rules entry.

The pack includes VFX_Impact_Dust and VFX_Impact_Sparks under VFX. The supplied projectiles use dust by default. The range uses sparks on a moving target’s first hit.

Damage Integration

Implement IDPT_FirearmDamageable on the hit object or one of its parents. The projectile passes the damage amount, world-space point, direction and source GameObject. Damage scales by the profile’s damage multiplier and the current speed relative to the initial speed, capped at full base scaling. Bayonet hits use the same receiver interface.

using DPT.Firearms.Runtime;
using UnityEngine;

public sealed class ExampleDamageReceiver : MonoBehaviour, IDPT_FirearmDamageable
{
    [SerializeField] private float health = 100f;

    public void ReceiveFirearmHit(float damage, Vector3 point, Vector3 direction, GameObject source)
    {
        health = Mathf.Max(0f, health - damage);
    }
}

To connect a health system in the Inspector, add DPT_FirearmDamageRelay. Use On Damage for a float callback, On Hit for a parameterless callback or its Hit C# event for the full hit data.

Projectile Impact Shake

On the projectile prefab, open Impact Shake on DPT_FirearmProjectile. Set Impact Shake Magnitude, Impact Shake Duration and Impact Shake Radius. A magnitude of zero disables impact shake. Add DPT_FirearmCameraShakeReceiver to each camera that should receive it. For shake when the weapon fires, configure Recoil & Firing Shake on the firearm.

Performance

For high pellet counts, use the ammo profile’s visual and audio pellet limits. Shorten effect and remnant lifetimes where they no longer matter. Keep the projectile collision layers focused and set contact caps for surfaces that allow multiple hits per shot.

Troubleshooting

SymptomCheck
A hit plays effects without changing health.Check for a damage receiver on the collider or a parent. If using the damage relay, connect its event to your health script.
The projectile passes through or misses a target.Check the collider, collision layers, range and any matched surface rule.
Penetration or ricochet never happens.Check rule order, limits, contact speed and the angle or exit requirements of the chosen reaction.
Changing a projectile seems to affect several weapons.Duplicate the projectile prefab if you want separate settings, then update the firearm or ammo profile reference.
A shot stops before the configured range.Look for a stopping surface. On Swept Projectile, check Fallback Lifetime, gravity and drag as well.
