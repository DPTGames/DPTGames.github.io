SMLE Mk III Example Setup

Worked example · Building and calibrating the included rifle with the general-purpose components

The SMLE is a worked example

This page shows one possible configuration of the reusable firearm system. The same core component supports other bolt actions, semi-automatic firearms and automatic weapons by changing the inspector settings and companion action implementation.

Recommended Scene Hierarchy

SMLE_MkIII_Complete  [DPT_Firearm, DPT_FirearmProceduralBolt, AudioSource]
├── Rifle_Model
├── Bolt_Transform  [procedural animation target]
├── Muzzle_Point   [empty +Z towards muzzle direction]
├── Ejection_Point [empty fixed to receiver]
├── Charger_Spawn  [empty near receiver charger bridge]
├── ADS_Camera_Pose [empty behind rear iron sights]
├── Hip_Camera_Pose [optional empty]
├── Bayonet_Visual  [optional child mesh]
│   └── Bayonet_Tip [empty +Z towards blade tip]
└── Muzzle_Flash_Light [optional Point Light, initially off]

Main Camera [separate scene root, not a child of the rifle]
The procedural bolt moves only the assigned Bolt_Transform. The muzzle, ejection point and camera pose markers should remain stationary relative to the rifle root. Do not parent the Main Camera under the rifle.

Placing Muzzle, Ejection and ADS Markers

  1. Muzzle Point: put the empty exactly at the muzzle. The blue +Z arrow must point downrange, as bullets are launched along MuzzlePoint.forward.
  2. Ejection Point: put the empty beside the right-hand receiver opening, not below the trigger, and assign the scene Transform to the firearm.
  3. Charger Spawn: put a separate empty near the top of the receiver. Assign it on the bolt component; falling back to Ejection Point works but is harder to align.
  4. ADS Camera Pose: put an empty just behind the rear notch, align its +Z arrow along the front sight and assign it on the demo controller.
  5. Bayonet Tip: put the empty at the end of the mounted bayonet with +Z pointing in the stabbing direction.

FBX / Local Axis Notes

  • The firearm root should have a predictable forward orientation. Unity convention is +Z forward, +Y up and +X right.
  • FBX import axis correction can create intermediate rotations. Check the actual child Transform axes in Unity rather than blindly reversing every Z value.
  • The procedural bolt has separate Invert Bolt Travel Z and Invert Bolt Rotation Z switches to correct imported bolt axes without changing projectile direction.
  • The ejection velocity uses Ejection Local Direction in the Ejection Point local space. To eject right, orient that marker accordingly.

Basic Verification

  1. Fire with a chambered round; the muzzle flash and shot sound should play and a round should be consumed.
  2. The bolt should unlock, retract, eject a casing at the receiver, feed a round and close.
  3. Fire all rounds, reload with one or two charger visuals and verify that the bolt closes after the final one.
  4. Hold right mouse to align ADS and check the sight picture with spread set to zero.
  5. Enable Bayonet Equipped, press F in the optional demo and verify the selected target receives a damage event.
Product positioning: These steps illustrate the included SMLE prefab. Customers can create a different weapon by configuring the same runtime APIs and supplying their own meshes, ammo, attachment transforms and input logic.
